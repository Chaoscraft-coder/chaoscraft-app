Privacy Policy: ChaosCraft Uploader

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Data the app accesses

When the account owner authorizes ChaosCraft Uploader through TikTok, the app receives:

  • basic profile information (display name, avatar, open ID), to show which account is connected (scope user.info.basic);
  • permission to upload and publish videos to that same account (scopes video.upload, video.publish);
  • the posting options TikTok returns for that account (for example, allowed privacy levels), so the owner can choose them before each post.

How it is used and stored

The access token and the data above are stored only on the creator's own computer and are used only to publish ChaosCraft's own videos to the connected account. They are not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising or analytics. The app does not read other users' data, messages, followers or comments.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the app is authorized. Revoking access in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions) makes them stop working; the local copy is then deleted. To ask for deletion of any data, write to the contact below.

Children

The app is not directed to children and is used only by the ChaosCraft creator.
