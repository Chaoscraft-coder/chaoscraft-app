1. What the app is

ChaosCraft Uploader ("the app") is a private publishing tool run by the ChaosCraft creator. It uploads original videos made by ChaosCraft to the ChaosCraft TikTok account, using TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API.

2. Who may use it

The app is used only by the ChaosCraft creator on their own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public. Anyone who authorizes the app must own the TikTok account they connect and must follow TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

3. Content

Only content the account owner made or has the rights to is published. The account owner chooses the caption, the privacy setting and the interaction settings (comments, Duet, Stitch) of every post, and is responsible for it.

4. Access and revocation

The app acts on a TikTok account only after the owner authorizes it through TikTok's login screen. The owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions.
